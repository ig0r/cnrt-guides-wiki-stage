CP Tech Center Guides

Root of the concept hierarchy extracted from the CP Tech Center guides and manuals corpus: the design, foundations, materials, construction, quality management, surface characteristics, evaluation, distress, preservation and sustainability topics those guides cover for concrete pavements, including concrete overlays. The scope is the guides, not the whole concrete pavement engineering domain.

CP Tech Center Guides reports covering “CP Tech Center Guides” also include the following subtopics: Pavement Structural Design, Pavement Foundations and Drainage, Concrete Pavement Overlays, Concrete Materials and Mix Design, Concrete Paving Operations, Pavement Surface Characteristics, Pavement Distresses, Concrete Pavement Preservation and Rehabilitation, Concrete Pavement Sustainability, Pavement Condition Assessment, and Quality Control and Quality Assurance Integration.
