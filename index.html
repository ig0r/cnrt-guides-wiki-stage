Concrete Overlays

Three cross-guide concept pages on the same subject, drafted from the CP Tech Center and FHWA concrete-pavement guide corpus. They exist to compare drafting approaches — what a wiki page adds over the guides themselves — not to rank the underlying sources. The site style is the concept wiki’s.

Concrete Overlays — practitioner (v2.1)Practitioner framing — role-based entry points, a six-step selection path, and a release-to-placement hold-point checklist.

Concrete Overlays — full-corpus (v3)Full-corpus synthesis — design values carrying the guide page they come from, the cross-guide disagreements, and the PennDOT Pub. 242 eligibility layer.

Concrete Overlays — merged (v4)The merge — v3’s technical depth on v2.1’s practitioner scaffolding, over one IEEE reference list of 29 sources.

Reading the citations

Citations are IEEE-numbered and link to the reference list at the foot of each page. A locator such as [1, p. 44] is the printed page of the cited guide; one such as [3, §12.2.C.2] is a section of PennDOT Publication 242.

The guide extractions behind these pages are not republished here — they are third-party documents totalling roughly a gigabyte. Reference entries still name the extraction each claim was read from, in muted text, so it can be traced in the working copy; those names are not links on this site.

Status

Staging build, draft status. Numeric values carry the guide page they came from and cross-guide disagreements are stated in place rather than silently reconciled — but nothing here substitutes for a project-specific pavement design or the governing agency specification.
